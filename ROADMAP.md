## 🤝 How to Contribute

We welcome contributions from the community! Here's how you can get involved:

1. **Check the good first issues** in GITHUB_ISSUES_BOOTSTRAP.md
2. **Read CONTRIBUTING.md** for setup instructions
3. **Join our community** discussions
4. **Submit PRs** for features you'd like to work on
5. **Report bugs** you encounter
6. **Suggest new features** that align with our roadmap

- 🔴 **Critical**: Must-have for next release
- 🟠 **High**: Important for upcoming milestone
- 🟡 **Medium**: Planned but not urgent
- 🟢 **Low**: Future consideration, nice-to-have
